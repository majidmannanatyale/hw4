

## Shopping & browsing
- **EPG-style catalogue.** Products are laid out in browsable, horizontally-scrolling rows by category (quarter-zips, crewnecks, hoodies, outerwear, tees), like a TV guide. 
- Sold-out sizes are struck through and nearly-gone sizes carry a small gold dot, so availability is obvious before the shopper commits to a size. 

## "Hot" / "Almost Gone" ribbons
- A ribbon appears on any product image that is selling fast (**Hot**) or nearly out (**Almost Gone**), everywhere the item shows up — catalogue, home page, chat, recommendations, and the product page.

## Add to bag + "Pairs well with"
- Adding an item opens a focused overlay that (a) confirms what went in the bag and the running total, and (b) suggests a few pieces that **pair** with it.
- The suggestions are a **different kind of apparel** than what was just added (a tee or crewneck alongside a hoodie, not another hoodie), so they feel like outfit-building rather than repetition — and they lean toward Hot / Almost-Gone pieces the shopper might otherwise miss. Each suggestion is clickable and jumps straight to that product.
- **Usability intent:** turn a single add into a considered second look, without a disruptive page change. The overlay closes on the X, the backdrop, or Escape.

## In-store pickup
- A clear "Request In-Store Pickup" button on each product page opens a short form: choose a date, and optionally say that **someone else** will collect it (which reveals a name field only when needed).
- **Usability intent:** meet shoppers who prefer to collect in person, with the smallest possible form — nothing is asked until it's relevant, and the flagship address is shown so expectations are set.

## The Concierge chat
- **Fast where it should be.** Simple greetings ("hi", "thanks") answer instantly, and a question that was already asked returns its answer immediately — the shopper isn't left waiting on the model for things that don't need it. A "typing…" indicator sets expectations when a real answer is being prepared.
- **Asks before guessing.** For vague requests ("show me sweaters") the Concierge asks one short clarifying question (style, colour, budget) rather than dumping a random selection, so the results are relevant the first time.

## Accounts
- Create-account and login forms use plain-language, gracious validation ("A name is no place for numerals, Your Grace"), confirm the password, and never expose anything sensitive.
- A logged-in shopper is greeted by first name and can log out from the header; logging out clears the chat so a shared device never shows the previous person's conversation.
- Users are treated as royalty, even in their messaging and greeting throughout the experience.
